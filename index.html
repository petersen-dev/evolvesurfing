The trip

Brazil Southernlands

A 10-night surf coaching trip based in Imbituba, Santa Catarina — between Praia do Rosa and Vila do Arvoredo. Consistent waves, minimal crowds, and real surf culture, far from the beaten track of the usual surf travel circuit.

This is not a surf holiday. It is a structured coaching program in one of Brazil's most beautiful coastal regions, with a maximum of four surfers per trip.

About Lin

Coached from
lived experience

I started surfing young and competed at international level, representing Argentina in two ISA World Junior Surfing Championships. That competitive background shaped how I think about progression, technique, and what it actually takes to improve in the water.

Over the past several years I have been coaching in Portugal, Costa Rica, Bali, Sri Lanka, and Brazil — working with surfers from intermediate to advanced levels, designing surf programs from scratch, and leading coaching teams across multiple camps.

My background in freediving and scuba adds another layer to how I coach. Breath control, ocean awareness, and comfort in challenging conditions are things I integrate naturally — because they have been part of my own training for years.
